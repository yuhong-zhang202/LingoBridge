# 「信息复用」图标重制方案 · 2026-07-20

> 用途：拿 Gemini 生成第三枚图标，替换 `public/icon-reuse.png`。
> 出方案：ux-reviewer（像素级实测，非目测）。

## 一、为什么要换（实测数据）

| | 描边占画布 | 主体占画布宽 | 描边色 | 出格色 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `icon-corpus-match` | **1.42%** | 89.9% | `#030303` | — |
| `icon-restructure` | **1.43%** | 94.3% | `#000000` | — |
| **`icon-reuse`（待换）** | **0.88%**（细 38%） | **77.5%**（小 12–17 点） | **`#2D2D32` 深灰蓝** | `#FFC9C9` 粉红、`#F5763F` 高饱和橙 |

三个维度全部出格。**粉红不在品牌色板**（全站只有暖橙 `#D4875A` 与薄荷 `#7BA699`）。
语义也弱：双向箭头通用语境读作「刷新/同步」，而该卡讲的是「练过的句子存进素材库、反复巩固」。

## 二、参考图的风格参数（新图必须对齐）

**精确色值**（像素统计，占比 >1%）：
- 蜜桃橙 `#FDB698`｜薄荷绿 `#A8DEBD`（两参考图 `#9BDCBA`/`#B1E0C0` 的中值，±5 容差）
- 米白/燕麦 `#E9D7BF`｜暖沙 `#E5C59E`｜纯白 `#FFFFFF`｜描边 `#000000`

**描边**：均匀等宽（无笔锋）、圆角端点与拐角、**每个色块独立完整闭合描边**、宽度 ≈ 画布宽 1.4%
**光影**：无。纯平涂，零渐变/投影/高光/纹理。层次**只靠黑描边 + 物件遮挡**
**背景**：完全透明（alpha=0）
**构图**：正方形画布，主体占 92%，四边留白约 4%

## 三、方案 A（首推）：错落卡片堆 · 无箭头

3 张圆角卡片扇形叠堆：后卡米白（−12°）、中卡薄荷（−5°）、前卡蜜桃（+4°），前卡面上 3 条白色圆角横条。

**为什么不放箭头**：前两枚都是**纯物件、零符号**（拼图块、对话气泡），加箭头会重蹈「抽象符号混进具象物件」的覆辙。卡片堆本身就是「积累 + 可反复取用」。
**与产品呼应**：`HomeDesktop.tsx:104` 的 `PreviewAnki` 正是两张白卡错落旋转叠放的形态，图标与真实 UI 同构。
白色内嵌条也复用了 restructure 气泡里那三个白方块的手法。

```
A flat vector sticker illustration icon of a stack of three overlapping rounded
rectangular cards, fanned out like a deck of flashcards.

COMPOSITION:
- Back card: rotated about -12 degrees, offset up-left, mostly hidden behind the others.
- Middle card: rotated about -5 degrees, partially visible.
- Front card: rotated about +4 degrees, fully visible, sitting on top and overlapping
  the other two.
- On the face of the front card only: three short horizontal rounded bars stacked
  vertically, representing lines of text. Nothing else on any card.
- All cards have generously rounded corners (corner radius roughly 12% of card width).
- The three cards clearly overlap each other so depth is communicated purely by
  occlusion, never by shadow.

EXACT COLORS (use these hex values precisely, no other colors anywhere):
- Front card fill: #FDB698 (soft peach)
- Middle card fill: #A8DEBD (soft mint green)
- Back card fill: #E9D7BF (warm cream / oatmeal)
- The three bars on the front card: #FFFFFF (pure white)
- All outlines: #000000 (pure black)

OUTLINE SPEC:
- Every shape, including each individual card and each white bar, has its own complete
  closed black outline.
- The outline is very thick and perfectly uniform in width, with no tapering and no
  brush variation — its thickness is approximately 1.4% of the total image width
  (a bold, chunky sticker outline).
- Rounded line caps and rounded corners everywhere.

STYLE:
Flat vector sticker illustration. Solid flat fills only. Completely two-dimensional.
Chunky, friendly, geometric. Cut-out sticker aesthetic.

BACKGROUND:
Fully transparent background (transparent PNG with alpha channel). No background color,
no backing plate, no frame, no circle behind the subject.

CANVAS AND FRAMING:
Perfect square canvas. The card stack is horizontally and vertically centered and
fills about 92% of the canvas width and 92% of the canvas height, leaving a small,
even margin of roughly 4% on all four sides.

STRICT NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS — do not include any of the following:
no gradient, no gradients of any kind, no drop shadow, no cast shadow, no inner shadow,
no highlight, no gloss, no glow, no bevel, no emboss, no 3D, no isometric, no perspective,
no texture, no grain, no noise, no paper texture, no sketch lines, no hand-drawn wobble,
no cross-hatching, no outline color other than pure black, no dark grey or navy outline,
no thin outlines, no pink, no magenta, no red, no purple, no blue, no saturated orange,
no neon, no high-saturation colors, no white background, no colored background,
no background circle or badge, no text, no letters, no numbers, no arrows, no icons
inside the cards, no logo, no watermark, no border frame, no photorealism.
```

## 四、方案 C（备选）：卡片堆 + 粗回旋箭头

若担心「光看卡堆读不出复用」用这个。**关键约束：箭头必须是有填充面积的实体形状，不是线条**，否则又回到出格状态；尺寸不超过卡堆 30%。

在方案 A 的 prompt 基础上，COMPOSITION 末尾追加：

```
- In the upper-right area, a single curved arrow that loops roughly three-quarters of a
  circle, ending in one solid triangular arrowhead. The arrow is a SOLID FILLED SHAPE
  with real surface area and its own black outline — it is NOT a thin drawn line or a
  hairline stroke. The arrow's overall size is no more than 30% of the card stack's size.
- The arrow slightly overlaps the corner of the back card so the elements read as one
  connected object.
```
EXACT COLORS 追加 `- Arrow fill: #A8DEBD (soft mint green)`
负面约束追加 `no thin line arrow, no hairline stroke arrow, no double-headed arrow, no refresh symbol, no sync symbol,`

## 五、方案 B（备选）：敞口收纳盒 + 立着的卡片

盒子（蜜桃）里立 3 张卡片（薄荷/米白/暖沙）露出上半截，前板遮住下部形成层次。
**风险：三个方案里最容易在 64px 小尺寸糊掉**（层次全靠遮挡、无阴影可用）。完整 prompt 见本轮 ux 评审原文，或按方案 A 的模板改主体描述。

## 六、出图后验收清单（任一条不过就重生成）

**必过项**
1. **背景真透明** —— 在深色背景上打开看有无白块。Gemini 常偷偷加白底
2. **描边是纯黑** —— 吸管取色，R/G/B 均 <20。出现 `#2D2D32` 这种深灰蓝直接废（**旧图就是这个病**）
3. **无品牌外颜色** —— 只允许 `#FDB698`/`#A8DEBD`/`#E9D7BF`/`#E5C59E`/`#FFFFFF`/`#000000`。**出现任何粉、红、亮橙，废**
4. **无渐变无投影** —— 放大 400% 看色块内部是否完全均匀
5. **三图并排比描边粗细** —— 缩到同尺寸眯眼看，**新图不能比另外两枚细**。宁可略粗
6. **缩到 64px 还认得出** —— 要能一眼读出「一叠卡片」
7. **无文字** —— 卡片上不能有字母/数字/假文字。Gemini 很爱自作主张加英文

**建议项**：居中且四边留白均等；三图光学重量接近；圆角调性与项目 `rounded-[16px]/[18px]` 一致

## 七、技术规格与压缩

**入库规格**：512×512 PNG 带 alpha（组件渲染 `w-16 h-16`=64px CSS，512 覆盖到 4x DPR 足够）
**文件名**：`icon-reuse-v2.png`（**别覆盖旧文件**，方便并排对比与回滚）

### 🔴 顺带一个与图标改版无关但很值的收益
三个 PNG 现在合计 **3.8MB**（1.4/1.5/0.84MB），这是**桌面首页首屏资源**。纯平涂 + 有限色数，量化到 8-bit 调色板肉眼无损、体积砍 90%+，**全部压完预计 <120KB**。

```bash
brew install pngquant imagemagick   # 一次性

# 新图：裁到主体边界 → 按 92% 占比补成 512 方形 → 量化压缩
magick icon-reuse-v2-raw.png -trim +repage /tmp/trim.png
magick /tmp/trim.png -resize 471x471 -background none -gravity center -extent 512x512 /tmp/sq.png
pngquant --quality=70-92 --speed 1 --strip --force --output public/icon-reuse-v2.png /tmp/sq.png

# 另外两张：同样重裁 + 压缩（统一 92% 占比，解决光学尺寸不等）
for f in icon-corpus-match icon-restructure; do
  magick public/$f.png -trim +repage -resize 471x471 \
    -background none -gravity center -extent 512x512 /tmp/$f.png
  pngquant --quality=70-92 --speed 1 --strip --force --output public/$f.png /tmp/$f.png
done

ls -lh public/icon-*.png   # 验体积
```

预期落在 15–40KB/张。仍超就把 `--quality` 下限降到 60。

**为什么三张都要重裁**：`object-contain` 按长边贴合 64px 框，留白越多看着越小。现状 89.9% / 94.3% / 77.5% ⇒ 统一到 92% 后光学尺寸才齐。
